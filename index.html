How to use Buyery Scout

  1. Tap Scan barcode. Allow camera access and point at the product barcode. If scanning is unsupported, type the barcode or product model.
  2. For products without a barcode, take a photo, then confirm the brand and model yourself. Photo recognition is not automatic yet.
  3. Enter the purchase price and condition. Tap Research product. Live eBay listings require a separately configured private API.
  4. Check comparable listings and enter a realistic expected selling price. Active asking prices are not verified sold prices.
  5. Choose an estimated parcel size, review postage and eBay fees, and correct them for your actual account and item.
  6. Only enter 90-day sold and active counts when you have matching, verifiable evidence; include its source and date.
  7. Tap Calculate. The large number shows estimated profit after entered costs. GOOD BUY requires at least £10 profit and 40% verified sell-through proxy; missing sales evidence means INSUFFICIENT DATA.
  8. Save your result to History. Export regularly: history is stored only on this device.
